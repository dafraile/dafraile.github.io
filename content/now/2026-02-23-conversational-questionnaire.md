Patient-reported outcome measures matter for care and research, but forms are tedious for patients and a burden for clinicians. With Mor Peleg I built a conversational agent on GPT-5 that collects the NIH Task Force minimal dataset for back pain by talking through topics, capturing several data items in one exchange instead of asking question by question.

Iterating with clinicians and a consumer panel produced a set of design principles for health data collection agents: flexibility of interaction style, personality calibration, confidence visualisation for data quality, patient safety constraints and interoperability. The preprint also covers the less glamorous parts: managing conversation length, ambiguous answers, and what breaks when the model version changes under you.
