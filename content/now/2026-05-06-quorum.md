Most AI assistants are a single voice. Quorum takes the opposite stance: a Session of archetypal agents deliberates in turns, the human Chair sets the agenda and rules, and nothing leaves the room unless the Chair signs it off. It supports clinical case review and general thinking sessions, and clinical text stays local until a local anonymiser clears it for any live model call.

The prototype came out of a clinical AI hackathon and is now supported by a Cosmos Institute grant on AI and human autonomy, which is exactly the question it is built around: can a group of models make a clinician's reasoning sharper without taking the decision away from them? The static seeded demo runs in the browser.
