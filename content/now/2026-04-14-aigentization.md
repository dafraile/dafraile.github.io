An experiment in making multi-agent orchestration legible by stealing the interface of a 4X game. Agents are units on a hex grid, work happens in turns, and "cities" are standing projects that queue tasks and accumulate output. It is a toy, but a useful one for thinking about what a human actually needs to see to supervise several agents at once.
