summary: An infinite 2D canvas where local LLMs grow haiku cell by cell, with sampling temperature set by position and a little dada prompt injection to keep it strange.
link: https://github.com/dafraile/bloom-canvas
tags: Play, Local LLMs, Generative art

Every cell on an infinite 2D grid is a haiku written by a local model, conditioned on its neighbours. Temperature is spatial, so one corner of the canvas is calm and literal and the other is feverish, and a small dada prompt injection seeds nonsense that propagates outward.
